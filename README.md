# gravity-holder
A 3D printed film holder for DSLR scanning.

> I wanted a scanning rig for my DSLR that would hold the film really still so that when I am cropping in my editor, I can just apply the same crop to the whole roll. I also wanted a holder that would be modular and have its own "bellows" to keep the ambient light out. This is the result of my first attempt.

I designed it to fit my `Canon EF 50mm f/2.5 Compact Macro` lens with an additional 13mm macro-tube attached to it.

However, you can adjust the dimensions of the tube that fits the lens to your own lens of choice pretty easily.

I made it so that you can add some magnets, but you don't really have to use them. If you have at least modestly heavy camera body or lens (or both), gravity will do its job just fine. I use Canon 600D and together with the lens it is plenty heavy for it to work (and it's not even a full-frame body).

It is supposed to fit the CineStill CS-Lite-Brite - it goes on top of it where their original cutout masks go.

It is all pretty modular. Pieces can be swapped and modified to fit tightly based on your own printer. I made it so that later, there could be a mask for medium format film too, but I haven't gotten to actually designing the different MF masks.


> I am already planning on designing a V2. I realized that it might have been better if the sliding mechanism was orthogonal to the film roll so that my fingers can avoid avoiding the film roll during sliding. Something that appeared obvious pretty much the first moment I had it assembled. The V2 is currently not in the works, it will take some time, I am working on other projects.


## How to Make it
Download the `.step` files from the `parts` directory and print them on your preferred setting. Alternatively, you can download the `Gravity Holder v1.3mf` file and load it into a compatible slicer.
It uses some standard M3 heat inserts and screws to hold everything together. You may use magnets but you don't have to.


## How to modify it
Download and import the Fusion 360 archive files from the `fusion` directory. You should be able to figure out the rest.
